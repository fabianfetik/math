  1. Sharp density thresholds for finite two-visit scheduling

    Preprint · 1 October 2026 · v1

    Each task must occur exactly twice, with a positive integer deadline bounding its first visit and the gap between visits. The paper gives sharp density bounds and a constructive scheduling algorithm.

  2. Real-period pinwheel scheduling at density 5/6

    Preprint · 28 September 2026 · v1

    Tasks must recur indefinitely under timing constraints given by real-valued periods. Schedules exist for every finite task family whose reciprocal periods sum to at most 5/6.
