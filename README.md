### [Sharp density thresholds for finite two-visit scheduling](https://fabianfetik.github.io/math/two-visits/)

Preprint · 1 October 2026 · v1 · cs.DM / math.CO / cs.DS

Each task must occur exactly twice, with a positive integer deadline bounding its first visit and the gap between visits. The paper gives sharp density bounds and a constructive scheduling algorithm.

[PDF v1](https://fabianfetik.github.io/math/two-visits/two-visits-v1.pdf) · [Details & material](two-visits/)

### [Real-period pinwheel scheduling at density 5/6](https://fabianfetik.github.io/math/real-pinwheel/)

Preprint · 28 September 2026 · v1 · cs.DM / math.CO

Tasks must recur indefinitely under timing constraints given by real-valued periods. Schedules exist for every finite task family whose reciprocal periods sum to at most 5/6.

[PDF v1](https://fabianfetik.github.io/math/real-pinwheel/real-pinwheel-5-6-v1.pdf) · [Details & material](real-pinwheel/)
